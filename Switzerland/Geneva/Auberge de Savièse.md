Address: Rue des Pâquis 20, 1201 Genève Switzerland

Area: Cornavin

Recommendation: Nothing to write home aboutm, but a good, decent value, spot for lunch if in Geneva

Dishes ordered: Malakoff (7 CHF), Raclette (24 CHF)

Charming operation with cozy alpine interior in international Geneva located right next to the lake. Their cheese quality is excellent and it's a good spot to try the classic swiss cheese dishes (raclette, fondue, malakoff etc.) without breaking the budget. Unfortunately did not get to sample any of their meat dishes and you will require two persons for the fondue, and it still is quite pricey as this is Geneva but its a pretty good shout for lunch if you're in the area and deadset on having local food like me.

The malakoff (fried cheese ball) was superb, perfectly crisp on the outside with a satisfying crunch on the base and a delightfully soft and gooey (though not oily/greasy) inside that had just the right amount of flavour. This was easily the star of the show. 

<img width="292" height="389" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-07-22 at 12 33 45" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e8eeb412-22ff-46db-ab40-8de5e31301cb" />

The raclette cheese was similarly nice and flavourful (if a bit greasy). I do recommend getting the meat version if you can afford it as the boiled potatoes etc. were a bit boring, though good quality.
